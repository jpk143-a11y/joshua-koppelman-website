Curiosity came first.

When I was 15, I started working at a local Italian restaurant, helping with cleaning and customer service. What caught my attention wasn’t just the food. It was the pace, the people, and all the small things that had to come together to make a restaurant work.

I wanted to learn in the kitchen, but at first I was told I was too young. I kept asking questions, staying longer, and learning whenever I could. Eventually, I earned the chance to cook and take on more responsibility.

I wanted to understand more than the job I was given.

That experience changed how I approach opportunities. When I care about something, I want to understand it, get involved, and show through my actions that I can do more.

The next chapter

Same curiosity.
A wider lens.

At the University of Miami, that same curiosity has led me into business, analytical work, leadership, and projects with teams. My summer at Restaurant Brands International connected those experiences back to the industry where it started.

I’m interested in where restaurants, consumer brands, and business development meet. I don’t have every next step mapped out. I do know I want to keep working on things that make me curious enough to go further.
